## 1. What this is

The owner asked how synchronised a customer record is between the Company/manifest Library on central and the ERP customer record through the Epicor/Kinetic connector. The 2026-09-01 discovery answered "zero sync in both directions", half by design; its haiClient half was later found to have measured a v1.67 checkout and was corrected on 2026-09-04 (`~/dev/hw/DISCOVERY-2026-09-01-erp-customer-seam.md`, addendum). This design is the go-forward the owner then ruled.

**Owner rulings (2026-09-04):**

1. **Artifacts are not for distribution.** A certificate of insurance may be read by the consuming agent, validated for compliance against requirements, and transitorily disposed of. A human may log in and review the document; local saving cannot be prevented. As general practice, artifacts do not flow.
2. **The attributes contained in them are fair game to update my own ERP with** — five million of liability coverage, a plant address.
3. **A new administrative surface in the haiWeb console**, under manifest management: **"Counterparty updates"** — a list with diffs between what my own systems contain and what the counterparty has represented via its manifest. Each row ends with the user selecting the value to keep.
4. **The flow:** the agent asks for the counterparty's attributes, sees a delta, reports it; the console lists it; the user decides; the agent syncs its updates and returns and applies the approved ones.
5. **A nightly job with variable check-in times.** Central picks each agent's slot with a random function so the fleet does not flood central at 01:00 GMT.
6. **A "Sync all now" button** at the global level of the counterparty attribute page.
7. **Each discrepancy row carries the counterparty's last-updated time for that attribute.** The human may not know the capture date of their own ERP value; seeing that the counterparty recently changed theirs helps them recognise the decision pattern.

**Owner decisions on this design (2026-09-04):** (1) street address: yes; **a plant address is not necessarily headquarters — headquarters may have multiple child plant locations**; (2) identity links are decided on the new tab too, with the existing PO-entry link view kept; (3) "take theirs" is a gated connector write — **be careful of permissions: an adopter may not enable the write, in which case an alert says "write not allowed, please update these records directly on your ERP"; the row stays dirty and clears on the next refresh once the pass sees the task was done manually in the ERP**; (4) the slot window is 00:00–06:00 UTC, one random minute per participant; (5) the buyer side (vendor link and vendor diff) ships in this cycle.

## 2. What this is not

Not a sync engine: no attribute value ever moves without a human decision, and nothing on central ever writes an ERP (D-149 stands). Not a store of ERP-owned facts on central: the one ERP value central sees lives inside a pending review row and is disposed of with it (§5.1). Not a document pipeline: no agent path fetches artifact bytes. Not a change to how orders read ship-to and terms (D-193 stands unchanged for orders). Not a re-validation of compliance on the agent: `ComplianceEvaluationService` on central remains the evaluator of requirements against disclosed holdings (`apps/core/src/services/compliance-evaluation-service.ts:32-59` `[core]`). Not a product-level surface: origin manifests, `compliance_snapshots` and the provenance change feed are a different "compliance" and stay untouched. Not a chat vocabulary change. Not a heartbeat: none exists (§3.2).

## 4. The ownership matrix

Every fact either side holds about a counterparty falls in exactly one class. The class fixes the system of record, whether the fact may cross into the adopter's ERP, and by what mechanism. The adopter's Epicor mapping then declares, per logical attribute, the Kinetic field it lands in — or none. **The nightly diff set = (what the counterparty discloses to me at my effective tier) ∩ (what my mapping targets).**

| class | system of record | crosses into my ERP? | mechanism |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Identity link** | a declared claim (`counterparty_erp_links` / `vendor_erp_links`) | n/a | match-or-flag at pair activation (§6.3); exact-name auto-link under `auto_high_confidence`, else a candidate list decided by a human; a claim is never overwritten silently (R-EXEC-25) |
| **B. Shared identity & contact** | the counterparty REPRESENTS; my ERP holds MY copy | yes, via review | nightly diff → keep-value → agent applies |
| **C. Locations** | the counterparty represents (HQ + child plants) | yes, via review | HQ ↔ the customer/vendor header address; each plant ↔ a customer ship-to / vendor purchase point (matched, or "not in your ERP") |
| **D. Declared commercial posture** | the counterparty represents; my ERP's `TermsCode`/`CurrencyCode` is my negotiated fact | yes, via review (informational-with-decision) | same; **D-193 is unchanged for orders** — the ERP value is still polled at the door |
| **E. Declared compliance attributes** | the Company Library (evidence = an artifact) | only where my mapping names a target (a UD column, a date field) | same; an unmapped attribute is not diffed; central's Scorecard stays the validator |
| **F. Artifacts** | the Company Library | **never** | console view only (inline, new tab — as today); no agent path fetches bytes; file reads become audited |
| **G. ERP-owned only** | my ERP | n/a | not diffed; poll at the door (D-193) |
| **H. Network facts** | central | never | untouched |

## 5. Security notes (the §4 discipline — register rows allocated by agent1)

1. **D-208 — the seam.** (i) The matrix above is the rule. (ii) **An ERP value crosses to central only as a transitory review snapshot** inside a pending `counterparty_attribute_updates` row (`mine_json`); it is read by exactly one central path (the owner's own console list), it is never disclosed to the counterparty, and it is **nulled when the row resolves** (applied / kept / converged / archived). This is a bounded carve-out amending D-193 ("no central column, no library element, no registry, no agent copy"): the value is not a column of record, not a resolver input, not retained. (iii) The owner sees only what the counterparty discloses at the owner's effective tier — the library read stays the single seam (D-49), unchanged; the public profile and manifest reads are the existing un-gated reads. (iv) **No agent path fetches artifact bytes** (ruling 1); the console keeps its inline viewer. (v) Counterparty artifact file reads become audited: `library.artifact.viewed { viewer, owner, artifact_id }` on `getCounterpartyArtifactFile` — today nobody records who read whose COI. (vi) The profile and manifest write paths emit the declared-but-never-fired `manifest.updated { fields[] }` and `upsertAttribute` emits a new `library.attribute.upserted`; per-field stamps ride the same writes (ruling 7).
2. **D-209 — counterparty-record ERP writes, the third write family.** `updateCustomerFields` / `updateVendorFields` (header fields named by the mapping) and `createCustomerShipTo` / `createVendorPurchasePoint` (a represented plant with no ERP row) are writes to the adopter's own ERP by the adopter's own agent, applied only to a row a human approved on the owner's console. They sit behind `counterpartySync.write` in the mapping (the `supplierPricing.write` shape) and the watched-first-write runbook (`docs/erp-write-enable.md`), which has never been opened. **When the write is not enabled: nothing is written, the approved row stays `approved` and is marked dirty, the console shows the alert "Write not allowed — please update these records directly in your ERP", and the next pass clears the row when it observes the ERP value now equals the approved value** (convergence). Idempotent on `(row id, run id)`; a partial failure names the row.
3. **Sync-now** rides the existing console→agent proxy (`POST /agent/api/tools/run_counterparty_sync`, allow-listed) under the existing haiCore service-client bearer, SSRF guard and dispatch ledger; one run in flight per agent (`already_running`, never a second pass). The tool takes no free text.
4. **Authorisation.** Console routes: `account_admin` (the profile edit's floor, D-197). Agent routes: the agent's existing scope + participant ownership from the JWT (`requireParticipantOwnership` — the `POST /participants/me/endpoint` shape); an owner reads and decides only its own rows (`owner_participant_id = caller`). Identity candidates (`candidates_json`) are the owner's OWN ERP rows and never leave the owner's view.
5. **Minimisation on the wire.** An observation carries `attribute_key, mine, theirs, theirs_updated_at, source` and, for identity rows, up to five candidates `{ erp_ref, id, name, city, state, score, matched_on }`. No credit, pricing or ship-to data of the counterparty's is ever observed (class G is not diffed).

## 6. Approach

### 6.1 Central data (haiCore, PG 0048)

- **`participant_locations`**: `id, participant_id, kind ('headquarters'|'plant'), label, address_line1, address_line2, city, state, postal_code, country, created_at, updated_at`; partial unique `(participant_id) WHERE kind='headquarters'`. Data step: one `headquarters` row per participant from `registration_requests.hq_*` where a matching request exists, else from `business_address_{city,state,country}` (lines empty). `participants.business_address_*` stay (read by search) and are kept in step by the profile write.
- **`counterparty_attribute_updates`**: `id, owner_participant_id, counterparty_participant_id, kind ('identity'|'attribute'|'location'), attribute_key, source ('profile'|'locations'|'library'|'manifest'|'erp'), mine_json (nullable — TRANSITORY), theirs_json, theirs_updated_at, candidates_json, observed_at, run_id, status ('pending'|'approved'|'applied'|'kept'|'converged'|'archived'), decision ('take_theirs'|'keep_mine'|'link'|null), decision_ref (the chosen ERP ref for `link`), decided_by, decided_at, dirty boolean default false, applied_at, apply_detail, archived_at, decided_theirs_hash`. Partial unique `(owner, counterparty, attribute_key) WHERE status IN ('pending','approved')`. Index `(owner_participant_id, status, observed_at DESC)`.
- **`counterparty_sync_state`** (one row per owner): `participant_id PK, slot_min smallint (0..1439), last_checkin_at, last_run_id, last_run_status, write_capabilities jsonb ({ customer_fields, vendor_fields, customer_ship_to, vendor_purchase_point }: boolean), agent_version, in_flight_since`.
- **`participants.profile_field_updated_at jsonb`** and **`counterparty_manifests.field_updated_at jsonb`** (field → ISO), written by `updateProfile` / `updateCounterpartyManifest` / `patchDeclarations` from an old/new diff.
- **`CounterpartyAttributeView`** gains `updated_at` and `source_tier` (additive; the column exists).
- Slot assignment: on the first `schedule` read, `slot_min = start + crypto.randomInt(window)` with `COUNTERPARTY_SYNC_WINDOW_START_UTC` (default `00:00`) and `COUNTERPARTY_SYNC_WINDOW_MINUTES` (default `360`); `POST /internal/counterparty-sync/reshuffle` (internal, the scheduler-trigger idiom) re-draws every slot.

### 6.2 Central routes (haiCore)

Agent-facing (agent scope + JWT participant ownership):
- `GET /api/v1/counterparty-updates/schedule` → `{ slot_utc: 'HH:MM', window: { start_utc, minutes }, last_checkin_at }` — assigns lazily.
- `PUT /api/v1/counterparty-updates/observations` body `{ run_id, trigger: 'boot'|'scheduled'|'manual', agent_version, write_capabilities, observations: Observation[] }` → for each observation: an existing `pending` row with the same `theirs` hash is refreshed (`observed_at`, `mine_json`); a different `theirs` replaces it; a `kept` decision whose `decided_theirs_hash` equals this `theirs` suppresses the row; an `approved` row whose `mine` now equals `theirs` becomes `converged` (the dirty row clears — decision 3); a row not observed this run for a still-linked counterparty becomes `converged`. Returns `{ approved: ApprovedRow[] (take_theirs and link decisions awaiting apply), next_slot_utc, counts }`. Stamps `counterparty_sync_state`.
- `POST /api/v1/counterparty-updates/:id/applied` `{ run_id, result: 'applied'|'failed'|'not_permitted', detail? }` → `applied` (+ `mine_json` nulled) / stays `approved` with `apply_detail` / stays `approved` + `dirty = true`.

Console-facing (`account_admin`, via haiWeb BFF):
- `GET /api/v1/counterparty-updates?status=pending|decided|all&counterparty=<id>` → `{ rows[], sync_state, counterparties[] }`.
- `POST /api/v1/counterparty-updates/:id/decide` `{ keep: 'mine' } | { keep: 'theirs' } | { link: '<erp_ref>' }` → `kept` (+ hash, `mine_json` nulled) / `approved` / `approved` with `decision_ref`.
- Sync-now: haiWeb calls the existing `POST /agent/api/tools/run_counterparty_sync` with `{}`; `TOOL_ALLOWLIST` gains the name. The tool answers `{ status: 'started'|'already_running', run_id? }`; the proxy's existing error shape covers `no_endpoint` / unreachable.
- Audit events as §5.1(v)(vi). No notification is added: the tab is the surface; the nightly library sweep's expiry notifications are untouched.

### 6.3 The agent (haiClient)

- **Client methods** (`haiwave-client.ts`): `getCounterpartyLibrary(id)`, `getParticipantProfile(id)` (public profile incl. `locations[]`), `getCounterpartySyncSchedule()`, `putCounterpartyObservations(body)`, `postCounterpartyUpdateApplied(id, body)`.
- **DuckDB 0023** creates `vendor_erp_links` (`erp_vendor_ref BIGINT PK (VendorNum), vendor_id (VendorID), participant_id, participant_name, linked_by, linked_by_name, matched_on, linked_at` + index on `participant_id`; `VendorErpLinkRepository` mirrors the 0021 repository) and `counterparty_sync_local_state` (agent-local, single row: `last_run_date, slot_utc, last_run_at, last_run_id, last_result_json`).
- **Connector contract** (optional methods, the presence-only idiom): `searchVendors?` / `getVendor?` **hoisted onto `ErpConnector`** (the Epicor implementation delegates to the existing `vendorSearch` / `vendorDetail` reads so a buyer without `supplierPricing` can still link; `ErpVendorHit` gains optional `address_lines[], city, state, zip, country, phone, email, terms_code`); `listCustomerShipTos?(erp_customer_ref)` (new resource `customerShipTos` → `Erp.BO.ShipToSvc/ShipToes?$filter=CustNum eq N`) and `listVendorPurchasePoints?(vendor_num)` (new resource `vendorPurchasePoints` → `Erp.BO.VendorPPSvc/VendorPPs?$filter=VendorNum eq N` `[swagger]` — the lane confirms the path against the dump); `getCustomer` gains `address_lines?[]` (Address1-3, today only synthesised into `ship_to`). **Writes** (D-209): `updateCustomerFields?(erp_customer_ref, patch)`, `updateVendorFields?(vendor_num, patch)`, `createCustomerShipTo?(erp_customer_ref, block)`, `createVendorPurchasePoint?(vendor_num, block)` — present on the Epicor connector only when the mapping's `counterpartySync.write` is true (the `supplierPricing.write` shape); the connector reports its `write_capabilities` from that flag and the mapping's declared targets.
- **Mapping**: a new optional `counterpartySync` block `{ write: boolean (default false), customerFields: Record<logicalKey, KineticColumn>, vendorFields: Record<logicalKey, KineticColumn>, valueMaps?: { payment_terms?: Record<string,string>, … } }`; the README documents the defaults of §4.1. Absent block = the defaults with `write: false`.
- **`CounterpartySyncService`** (`packages/reference-agent/src/services/counterparty-sync/`), `runOnce(trigger)`, one in flight (a second call answers `already_running`):
  1. `GET /connections/active` → partners with `relationship_state === 'trading_pair'` (the `readTradingPairPartners` filter that exists).
  2. **Link step**, per partner, per side the agent plays (a partner can be both a customer and a vendor): no link row → `searchCustomers({ text: partner.name })` / `searchVendors(...)` → for each hit run the existing exact-arm rule against THIS partner (`rankVendors(hit.name, [partner])`); **exactly one hit with an exact arm** and mode `auto_high_confidence` ⇒ `upsert` a link (`linked_by: 'agent'`, `matched_on` recorded); otherwise ⇒ an `identity` observation carrying up to five ranked candidates + a work item `counterparty_unlinked` (chase; `dedupKey counterparty-unlinked:<side>:<participant_id>`; `subjectType null`); the item resolves when a later pass finds the link (`resolveBySubject` by dedupKey). A partner whose link's `participant_name` differs from `partner_name` gets the name refreshed on the link row (display copy only).
  3. **Diff step**, per linked partner: theirs = `getParticipantProfile` (incl. locations) + `getCounterpartyLibrary` (only `disclosed_element_keys` with an `attribute`) + `getParticipantManifest`; mine = `getCustomer({ erp_customer_ref })` + `listCustomerShipTos` (seller side) / `getVendor` + `listVendorPurchasePoints` (buyer side). For every logical key the mapping targets: normalise (trim, case-fold, collapse whitespace; amounts as numbers; terms through `valueMaps`; addresses as the tuple `(lines joined, city, state, postal, country)`) and emit an `attribute` observation when unequal. Locations: HQ ↔ the header address; each plant ↔ the ERP location row matched by exact/concat name, else by `(city, postal_code)`; an unmatched plant ⇒ a `location` observation with `mine: null` ("not in your ERP"); an ERP location with no represented plant is NOT observed (class G). `theirs_updated_at` = the per-attribute stamp central now exposes.
  4. **Report**: one `PUT …/observations` with every row (idempotent per `run_id`).
  5. **Apply**: for each returned approved row — `take_theirs` on an attribute ⇒ the matching write when present ⇒ `applied`; on a `location` with `mine: null` ⇒ the create write; `link` ⇒ `linkByOperator`-shaped upsert (`linked_by 'operator'`, `linked_by_name` = `decided_by`) — no ERP write; no write capability ⇒ `not_permitted` (the row goes dirty on central; the console alerts). Every result is acknowledged individually.
  6. Persist `counterparty_sync_local_state` (date BEFORE the pass — the quote-eval discipline), re-read `next_slot_utc`.
- **`CounterpartySyncScheduler`** on the `QuoteEvalScheduler` idiom: boot pass (`trigger: 'boot'`), then due when `lastRunDate < today(UTC)` and `now ≥ slot`; the slot comes from `GET …/schedule` at boot and from every observations response; central unreachable ⇒ the last persisted slot; no slot ever known ⇒ the boot pass still runs (it is the first check-in) and the scheduled pass waits for a slot (logged once).
- **MCP tool `run_counterparty_sync`** (`src/mcp/tools/run-counterparty-sync.ts` + the barrel + the registrations array): input `{}`; output `{ status: 'started'|'already_running', run_id? }`; `readOnlyHint: false, destructiveHint: false`; the pass runs detached (the tool answers at once), never blocks the MCP session.
- **Work item** `counterparty_unlinked` joins `ChaseItemType` (`queue/types.ts`); detail = the partner, the side, the candidates, a link to the PO-entry link view; Ignore/close as any chase item.
- **Mock ERP**: `customers.json` rows gain `address_lines, email, phone, terms_code`; new `vendors.json`, `ship-tos.json`, `purchase-points.json` (flat maps, the `customers.json` precedent); the mock implements the four writes when `ERP_MOCK_WRITE=true` (the existing mock write gating), reporting `write_capabilities` accordingly.

## 7. The flow, end to end

1. **A pair activates.** Nothing crosses the wire (as today). At the seller's next pass (boot, slot, or Sync all now) the partner has no link: one exact-name customer hit under `auto_high_confidence` links it; otherwise the tab shows "Acme Corp — not matched to a customer record" with the candidates and the operator's queue carries `counterparty_unlinked`. The admin picks a candidate on the tab, or the operator links in PO-entry; either way the next pass sees the link and the item resolves. The buyer's agent does the same for the vendor side.
2. **The nightly pass.** At its slot the agent diffs every linked partner and reports; the tab lists "General Liability Limits — yours $3,000,000 · theirs $5,000,000 · they updated 2 days ago", "Headquarters address — yours … · theirs …", "Plant — Toledo, OH — not in your ERP". The admin keeps or takes each.
3. **Take theirs, write enabled.** The next pass (or Sync all now) applies the value to the ERP, acknowledges, and the row shows applied; central nulls the snapshot.
4. **Take theirs, write not enabled.** The pass answers `not_permitted`; the row goes dirty; the alert names the records to update in the ERP by hand. When the admin has done so, the next pass observes mine == theirs and the row converges (decision 3).
5. **Keep mine.** The row is kept; the same represented value never re-surfaces; a NEW represented value does.
6. **Sync all now.** The button calls central's tool proxy; the agent starts a pass and answers `started`; the tab polls until the run id changes; an unreachable agent shows the proxy's error in the banner.
7. **The counterparty edits its profile or library.** Central stamps the field, fires `manifest.updated`; nothing is pushed; the next pass on every partner's side picks it up with its last-updated time (ruling 7). Sync all now covers urgency.

## 8. Errors, idempotency, degradation

- **Absence is not evidence** (the D-204 discipline): central unreachable ⇒ the pass logs and ends, state untouched, slot unchanged; the ERP unreachable ⇒ no observations for that partner (no `converged`, no dirty clearing); a partner missing from `/connections/active` ⇒ its rows are left alone (the pair may be suspended, not ended).
- **Never throws out of the pass**; per-partner failures log and skip; per-row apply failures are acknowledged `failed` with an authored detail (the ERP's text never reaches a person; the ledger idiom).
- **Idempotency**: observations keyed by `(owner, counterparty, attribute_key)` with the `theirs` hash; `run_id` on every write; applies acknowledged per row; the create-write probes for an existing location row by name before creating (the `stampField` probe idiom).
- **Ambiguity is never resolved silently**: two exact identity hits ⇒ a candidate list; a plant matching two ship-tos ⇒ "matched ambiguously" with both candidates and no auto-apply.
- **Broad degradation**: no `counterpartySync` block ⇒ defaults, `write: false`; no vendor reads ⇒ the buyer side reports nothing and the tab says so once per counterparty; no `listCustomerShipTos` ⇒ plants are observed as "not comparable" rows with no action.
- **The stale-checkout class**: every lane measures on its own worktree; the 09-01 discovery is the exhibit.
